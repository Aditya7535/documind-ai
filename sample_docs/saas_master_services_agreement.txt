# MASTER CLOUD SERVICES AND DATA PROTECTION AGREEMENT (MSA)

## SECTION 1: SERVICES AND SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS (SLA)
1.1 Service Availability: The Provider warrants that the Cloud Services shall maintain an Uptime Service Commitment of at least 99.95% during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
1.2 Service Credits: In the event Provider fails to meet the 99.95% SLA, Customer shall be entitled to the following financial service credits:
- 99.0% to 99.94% Uptime: 10% monthly subscription credit.
- 95.0% to 98.99% Uptime: 25% monthly subscription credit.
- Less than 95.0% Uptime: 50% monthly subscription credit.

## SECTION 2: DATA PRIVACY, SECURITY AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE USAGE
2.1 Customer Data Ownership: Customer retains all rights, title, and intellectual property ownership in Customer Data and input documents uploaded to the platform.
2.2 AI Model Training Exclusion: Provider explicitly covenants that Customer Data, uploaded files, vector embeddings, and prompt interactions will NOT be used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve any public, third-party, or foundational foundation models (including AWS Bedrock or Anthropic models).
2.3 Encryption Standards: All data in transit shall be encrypted using TLS 1.3 or higher. All data at rest within Amazon S3 and Amazon OpenSearch Serverless shall be encrypted using AES-256 with AWS Key Management Service (AWS KMS) customer-managed keys (CMK).
2.4 Compliance: Provider maintains SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance certifications.

## SECTION 3: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
3.1 Direct Damages Cap: Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breaches of Section 2 (Data Privacy), neither party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
3.2 Intellectual Property Indemnity: Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claims alleging that the Cloud Service infringes any copyright, patent, or trade secret.
